Shipped in [KAIF 2.7 — Audited KAIF](https://github.com/MikalaiKryvusha/KAIF/releases/tag/v2.7). Both halves of the ticket, and the "+1" from the second deployment:
1. **Authorship of a decision** is now a canon rule (`AGENT_GUIDE.md` → "Authorship of a decision", both layers): every recorded decision carries its author — `[OWNER] "<verbatim>" · <date>` (or the interview address) vs `[AI]`; **"do as you see fit" is a MANDATE**, recorded as `[AI] by mandate — "<the owner's words>"`, never as the owner's decision; **"not to be revisited" belongs to `[OWNER]` decisions only**; the source of truth about the owner's words is the chat and `interviews/` — everything else is a retelling.
2. **The guard you asked for:** the new optional module `.kaif/tools/kaif-attribution-lint.mjs` — `check [paths…] [--write-baseline]` / `selftest`. A line that attributes a decision or an order to the owner with no verbatim quote, no interview address and no decision number within ±2 lines is DEBT, counted against a baseline that only shrinks (adopt once with `--write-baseline`); a line signed `[AI]` (or your localized pair) is the agent's own decision and is not judged; the declared exception is `<!-- attribution-ok: <where the quote lives> -->`. Your P1 line is the selftest's fixture: red as written, green with «давай как ты считаешь» beside it.
3. **Provenance marks are legal outside the canon** (the "+1" — "(my taste)"): `kaif-provenance check` no longer refuses `[AI]…[/AI]` in a draft, an interview, a table you bring to the owner; marks stay REQUIRED in `canonArtifacts`; `report` lists the drafts as a separate group; the canon says it plainly — **a pronoun is not a provenance mark**.
4. `/fable-judge` hunts "an agent decision worn as the owner's word". Thank you for the measurement (430 of 1083) — it is what turned a paragraph into a guard. Closing with the release.
