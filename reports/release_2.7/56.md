You are right — the only check of a document used to be its show, and the show called you. Fixed in [KAIF 2.7 — Audited KAIF](https://github.com/MikalaiKryvusha/KAIF/releases/tag/v2.7): the generator has a separate door, `--check <doc>` — parse, pre-flight and render self-check with no page, no sound, no call and no showing recorded; it prints what it recognised and what it did not, and refuses with exit code 3. `/interview` step 3c now prescribes exactly this door, and the help text names `--no-open` honestly: "raises the page and calls".
