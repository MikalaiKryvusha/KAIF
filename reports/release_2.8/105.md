Shipped in [KAIF 2.8 — Noble KAIF](https://github.com/MikalaiKryvusha/KAIF/releases/tag/v2.8):

- **`TESTING_FRAMEWORK.md` step 6 — "Hunt the reproduction"**, between the control case and filing: a defect or a reported phenomenon that did not reproduce on the first attempt is varied over named axes — data and state · position · timing and races · entry point · fresh vs accumulated account · stage vs production · network — and every attempt is written down. Exit: the steps reproduce it, or the report says "not reproduced" and lists at least three variants tried, each with its outcome — one attempt is never a verdict.
- **Step 7 — "File defects in the defined shape"** names the tester's report a developer reads: Description · Steps to reproduce · Expected result · Actual result, plus Build · Environment · Evidence and the severity/priority the tracker takes; the steps are the user's path in the product, never state assembled through a back door.
- **`/report-bug` template C, "tester's bug report"** — the four sections and the three lines. An unreproduced defect carries `**Status:** not reproduced after the variants below` and `## Reproduction hunt`, a table `| # | variant (axis: value) | outcome |` of at least three rows.
- **The check:** `node .kaif/tools/kaif-testrun-lint.mjs bug <report.md>` — a second genre of the run-report linter, not a new module. Five rules, each named on exit 1: `missing-section` · `empty-section` · `missing-line` · `steps-not-a-path` (the steps are not a numbered list) · `hunt-too-short` (the report says the defect did not reproduce and the hunt table has fewer than three rows with an outcome). Headings may be H2 or H3, numbered, plural or bold (`## **Description**`); a label alone on its line takes its value from the next line. `bug --keywords` prints what each language accepts; for Russian: `## Описание · ## Шаги воспроизведения · ## Ожидаемый результат · ## Фактический результат`, the hunt `## Охота за шагами`, the lines `**Сборка:** · **Окружение:** · **Улики:**`.

What your side does: your deployment is translated wholesale, so the update does not insert step 6, step 7 or template C into your translated files — they arrive in the update task as deltas for a hand merge, each marked "NEW module in this release (not inserted — the file is translated)". The task's `policy-changes` item carries the line "[2.8] A defect that did not reproduce is not a verdict" — a rule change your owner sees and decides on. Your own four-section rule and the section guard of your ticket tool stay yours; the shipped check judges the markdown report before it goes to the tracker.

Boundaries, said plainly: the check judges the FORM. `steps-not-a-path` sees a numbered list, not whether the steps are the client path or API calls — that stays the tester's judgement under step 7. It does not know whether a hunt row was really run or which axis it varied. The verdict "not reproduced" is read from the `**Status:**` / `**Статус:**` line when there is one — write it there; without it, from negation-around-the-stem patterns of both languages, outside inline code and outside the hunt table. A list item is not a variant: only a row of a real table with its outcome filled counts. Two languages ship, English and Russian. The developer report from your ticket was not on the origin's machine, so the functional runs used reports of its form built from the shipped template, and the hunts were synthetic fixtures.
