Shipped in [KAIF 2.8 — Noble KAIF](https://github.com/MikalaiKryvusha/KAIF/releases/tag/v2.8):

Your row was invisible because any date on a line made the scan treat it as a journal entry. In 2.8 the stale-claims scan (`scanStaleClaims` in `.kaif/kaif-core.mjs`) no longer judges a line by its shape:

- **A date hides a line only when it stands outside parentheses.** In `` | **Версия KAIF** | `2.1` «Strong KAIF» (релиз от 2026-07-31) | `` the date is inside a parenthesis. The line is judged, and the pair "KAIF … 2.1" makes it a claim.
- **The deployment record's own row is judged whatever dates its other cells carry.** A table row whose label names the framework version is the record itself, never a journal entry. That covers `KAIF version`, `Версия KAIF`, and the label used in each of the ten language templates of `KAIF_FRAMEWORK.md`. The release court found that the first build knew only the English and Russian labels, and that was fixed.
- **A claim is a pair:** the framework's word and a version that belongs to it, with no conjunction and no other product name between them. `KAIF и Acme Space 2.0` stays silent, while `2.1 «Strong KAIF»` is named.
- **The hit line.** A row that names a version older than the one just replaced is shown as `KAIF_FRAMEWORK.md:<line> — <the row> (asserts 2.1)`.
- **The marker for a correct row.** A row that is correct by design takes `<!-- KAIF-VERSION-OK: reason -->` INSIDE the row. A marker on the line above no longer excuses the row below it, because that line is another row.
- **NEW command `node .kaif/kaif-core.mjs stale-claims`** re-runs the scan read-only at any time. The interval defaults to your last update receipt; use `--from`/`--to` to name another.

What you do: after the 2.8 update, run `node .kaif/kaif-core.mjs stale-claims`, or tick `checkpoint stale-claims`, which the delivered core executes. On the `update` route the task item itself is written by your outgoing 2.7 core with the old rule. On this hop the new rule reaches you through the checkpoint and the command, and from the next update on through the item as well. Your row now names 2.7. Once you are on 2.8, that is exactly the kind of line the new scan names.

  - C14: the record row under the labels of the eight other language templates is named. It showed "0 of 8" on the build before that fix.
