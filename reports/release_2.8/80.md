Shipped in [KAIF 2.8 — Noble KAIF](https://github.com/MikalaiKryvusha/KAIF/releases/tag/v2.8):

- **One line instead of 88.** `node .kaif/tools/kaif-experience-lint.mjs check` now folds every failure entry that carries no `class:` and stands before the journal's first classed entry into ONE warning: `⚠ no-class: N failure entries carry no class: and predate the first classed entry (EXP-…, <date>) — outside the field rules; list them: --verbose (classify from the newest end: the fold shrinks as you go)`. `--verbose` lists them by id, as you proposed. "Before" is the entry's place in the journal, read in the direction most of its dated neighbours show. An unclassed failure written after the first classed entry, or dated later than it, keeps its own warning, which is the real finding your ticket asked to keep. A journal whose order its dates cannot tell (no dates, or a single date) is not folded at all.
- **And the one line can go too.** `check --write-baseline` records the inherited debt once, in `.kaif/experience-lint.baseline.json` next to the journal. A plain `check` then reads that file by itself and names the debt in its summary line, with no per-entry warnings. A later write only shrinks the baseline and never takes in a new entry. A repeated class is still judged: the baseline does not silence the rule that matters. `/end-chat-soft` Step 1 now names the command and asks you to commit the file.

On your side: nothing is required. The fold works as soon as the module is updated. For a silent closing, run `node .kaif/tools/kaif-experience-lint.mjs check --write-baseline` once after the update and commit `.kaif/experience-lint.baseline.json`. The optional "classify the last N" helper did not ship. The fold line says where to start instead (the newest end).
