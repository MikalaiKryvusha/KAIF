Shipped in [KAIF 2.8 — Noble KAIF](https://github.com/MikalaiKryvusha/KAIF/releases/tag/v2.8): the change your ticket proposed, nearly word for word.

- **The rule.** `AGENT_GUIDE.md` → "Authorship of a decision" gains a new rule, **"The rulebook takes the rule, not the quote"**:
  - An owner's standing instruction enters the guide or the house-rules file as a strict rule: imperative, numbered, with its exceptions.
  - It carries ONE provenance line: `[OWNER] <date> · <where the verbatim lives>`. That place can be the commit that recorded his words verbatim first, the interview, or the row in the decision log.
  - His words stay at that source. A block of raw chat messages inside the rulebook is a defect.
- **The route you found missing.** `/fix-vision` step 3 now sends a standing working rule to `HOUSE_RULES.md`, section "The owner's standing rules", in exactly that form. If there is no file yet, create it with `cp .kaif/_house-rules-template.md HOUSE_RULES.md`. The guide's "Notes from the human" section keeps only what changes the framework's own procedure. The skill also now uses the section name the template uses (in 2.7 it said "Notes from the owner").
- **The shape on paper.** The shipped skeleton `.kaif/_house-rules-template.md` §1 shows the form: `### R1. <rule title>`, then numbered steps, then `**Exception:**`, then one `[OWNER] … · <commit <hash> · interview #NNN, QN · decision #NN>` line.
- **The lint no longer punishes that form.** In `kaif-attribution-lint`, a commit address on the attribution's own line now counts as its source: the word "commit" next to a hash of at least 7 hex characters that contains a digit. Your own field form, `[OWNER] 2026-09-22 · verbatim in commit 6411a9ed`, gave 1 finding under the 2.7 module and gives 0 under 2.8. "Verbatim in the commit above" (no hash), a bare hash, "recommit 1234567" and a hash on a neighbouring line are still findings.

On your side: the update task carries the module changes. Your rewrite into five numbered rules already has this form. The existing «Дословно» sections stay your owner's call, as your ticket says.

Not in 2.8: the optional canon-lint guard that would flag a blockquote of chat messages in a guide section. No lint flags such a dump today. The rule in the guide and in `/fix-vision` holds that line, and nothing checks it by machine.
