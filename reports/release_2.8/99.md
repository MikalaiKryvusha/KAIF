Shipped in [KAIF 2.8 — Noble KAIF](https://github.com/MikalaiKryvusha/KAIF/releases/tag/v2.8): the cheap half of your ticket, taken into this release by the KAIF owner's answer to the origin's interview #035 (Q2). The price of entering a chat is now printed, and the voice portrait loads only what writing needs.

**1. The entry cost is printed.** `node .kaif/kaif-core.mjs check` prints one reference line: `ℹ entry cost: /resume reads N re-read core document(s)[ + HOUSE_RULES.md] ~ Nk tokens — X % of a 1M-token model window (reference, never a stop; ~ 2.5 ASCII / 1.9 other characters per token)`.
- The two rates are named constants. The first is 2.5 characters per token for ASCII, from the model page ("1M tokens ≈ 2.5M characters"). The second is 1.9 for any other character, taken from your own measurement: a mostly Cyrillic core document of 140,597 bytes came to 40,880 tokens.
- There is only one calibration point, so the line says "~".
- The farewell of `/end-chat-soft` now carries this line in the owner's language, so he sees the price at every closing.

**2. The portrait loads its writing sections.** A bare `node .kaif/tools/kaif-voice-lint.mjs load` now prints the head and the sections needed for writing, with their size in lines and tokens:
- It loads §0 · §1 · §2 · §5 · §6 · §7 with their subsections, including the §2-C lexicon.
- For every section it leaves out, it prints the section's weight and a ready ASCII-only `--sections` regex.
- `--all` loads the whole portrait as before. `check` and the load witness are unchanged.
- A portrait whose writing sections carry no numbers is loaded whole, and the summary line says so.

On your side: nothing to merge. Keep your portrait's section numbers as the skeleton has them. When a text needs a module the plain load left out, load it with the regex the summary printed.

Not in 2.8; these are on the list for the next version:
- a closing gate on the share of the model window (your `--gate-ritual`);
- an hourly refresh that re-reads a one-page digest instead of the whole core;
- the rule that a new canon rule enters only by displacing an old one.

Limits of what did ship:
- The line is information. It never stops a closing, and the line budgets remain the gate's unit.
- It names a 1M-token window in words and does not check which model is running.

The shipped canon itself became lighter. Its rules' stories — the ticket, the owner's quote, the version — moved into `.kaif/KAIF_REFERENCE.md` §17 "Why the canon says so". The guide template went from 1200 lines to 1080.

Proof:
- Entry cost, sandbox suite `s16`: the thousands and the share equal a separate recount from disk, and a house-rules file of known weight adds its tokens. Both asserts are red on the 2.7 core. Mutants M19 (a 200k window under the words "1M") and M20 (the line not printed) go red on exactly those asserts.
- Entry cost, real run on copies of your nine core documents (the project was only read): `~ 225k tokens — 23 % of a 1M-token model window`. Your ticket measured 810,567 bytes. By the time of the run the documents had shrunk to 765,724 bytes.
- Portrait: `kaif-voice-lint selftest` passes 57 cases. Sandbox suite `s26` passes 88 checks (re-run 2026-09-26), and on the 2.7 module exactly the 7 new asserts are red. Six mutants each go red on their own asserts.
- Portrait, real run on copies of eight real portraits: each of the 142 printed regexes loaded exactly its own section. Yours loads about 37k of about 170k tokens, 4.5 times smaller.
