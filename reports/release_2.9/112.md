Shipped in [KAIF 2.9 — Distilled KAIF](https://github.com/MikalaiKryvusha/KAIF/releases/tag/v2.9):

- **`check <path> --write-baseline` no longer erases the debt of other files.** The write goes through `nextBaseline()` in `kaif-attribution-lint`. Entries of files outside the scanned paths stay as they were, and only entries inside the scope are replaced by this run's counts. The write message names them: "N entries of files outside <path> kept as they were". This is the merge form of your proposed fix.
- **The "no longer found — rewrite it" hint counts only the scope.** `prunableEntries()` counts recorded entries inside the paths of the run. Your one-file `check AUTHOR_STYLOMETRY.md` no longer reports the whole baseline as "92 entries no longer found".
- **A third identical line is a NEW finding (your correction comment).** The baseline now stores a count per key (`file:sha16(line)` → number). `freshFindings()` treats every copy above the recorded count as new: "the (count+1)-th copy of a recorded line is NEW". Your repro, two baselined lines plus a third, now exits 1 instead of `new 0 · debt 3`. Fixing one of two identical lines still shows as `debt 1`, as your comment measured.

What your side does: after the update, run one full-scope `node .kaif/tools/kaif-attribution-lint.mjs check --write-baseline` and commit the file. The counts start from that write; see the boundary below.

The key is still per file and text, so a line moved within its file keeps its key.
