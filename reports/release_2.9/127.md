- **2 — the follow-up label.** The Russian pack writes `Ответ (дополнение, …):`.
- **3 — the place of a follow-up.** It goes to the END of the question's block, below an "applied" mark line, never right under the answer.
- **4 — `decision.json` across revisions.** The spec and the code now say one thing. The heading of §3 reads "Records — three files, derived names; a record is replaced only by a NEW revision of the document (the table below)", and the table says: the saves of ONE page merge into the decision, and a new revision of the document starts a new decision. An earlier decision is not lost: the archive keeps a copy per save and is never rewritten. The renamed heading is declared to the update as a pair, so your copy of the spec gets one heading, not two.
- **5 — a comment under an empty `**Answer:**`.** HTML comments are cut before the answer is judged, so a comment alone leaves the question live.
